**H2 · Free-shipping progress bar in the bag** — Because many baskets end just below €100, we believe showing progress towards free shipping ('€18 to free shipping') in the bag will make shoppers add another item, raising items per order and revenue per visitor. Change: Progress bar with the remaining amount at the top of the slide-in bag and cart; turns green with 'Free shipping unlocked' at €100.

**H1 · Mobile bag: make checkout the obvious next step** — Because mobile shoppers see a long bag with the Checkout button pushed down and no reassurance, we believe a sticky Checkout button with delivery and returns reassurance in the bag will raise mobile checkout starts. Change: Sticky footer in the slide-in bag on mobile: subtotal, prominent Checkout button, and a line with '30-day free returns · delivery in 1–2 days'.

**H4 · Sticky Add to bag on mobile product pages** — Because mobile shoppers scroll into copy, specs and reviews and lose the Add to bag button, we believe a sticky Add to bag bar (price + button) that appears once the main button scrolls away will raise mobile add-to-bag rate. Change: Bottom sticky bar on mobile PDPs with product name, price and Add to bag, shown when the main button is out of view; asks for size if none selected.

**H3 · 'Complete your kit' on product pages** — Because shoppers buying a main item are not shown matching accessories, we believe a 'Complete your kit' row with quick add will raise items per order. Change: Row of 3 matching accessories under Add to bag, each with image, price, stars and a quick '+ Add' button.

**H5 · Helpful zero-result search page** — Because a dead-end search page gives shoppers nowhere to go, we believe suggesting related categories and popular products on zero-result searches will keep them browsing and raise product views from search. Change: Zero-result page with 'We don't sell tents yet', links to related categories (Backpacks, Accessories) and a row of 4 bestsellers.
